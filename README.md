# Tire Width Lab

An interactive calculator that shows why wider road bike tires can be faster, and where that stops being true.

**Use it:** https://mechanicalrandolph.github.io/tire-width-lab/

Set your speed, weight, road surface, tire width, rim width, pressure and elevation. The page splits your tire losses into:

- **Rubber flexing** (casing hysteresis), fitted to the Bicycle Rolling Resistance GP5000 23/25/28/32 mm drum tests
- **Bouncing / vibration** (Silca's "impedance" breakpoint idea)
- **Tire aero drag**, including the "rule of 105" penalty when the tire is wider than the rim

Two charts show the limits: the best pressure for your width, and the fastest width for your speed and road.

This is a simplified model. It shows the shapes and trade-offs, not exact watts for a specific tire.
